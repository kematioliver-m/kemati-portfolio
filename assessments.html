Assessment Philosophy

Evidence Over Guesswork

"Assessment should never be a surprise at the end of a unit. Every lesson I plan closes with an Evaluate stage: a deliberate, low-stakes check that tells me, in the moment, whether the lesson actually landed. The graded assessments that follow are never the first time I find out."

Two Layers, Working Together

Across all four placements, assessment happens on two levels. Formative checks (questioning, circulation, show-of-hands, the Evaluate stage of every 5E lesson) happen every single session and shape what I do next, not just what grade a student receives.

Summative assessments sit at the end of a unit, once students have had genuine practice with everything they're being asked to demonstrate. The EXCED FIT Lab placement below shows this most clearly: four unit assessments, each worth 10%, each landing only after a full run of scaffolded practice sessions.
