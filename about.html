Biography

Who I Am

I am a Business Systems Analyst at Guardian Life Limited and an Adjunct Lecturer at the University of the Commonwealth Caribbean (UCC), currently pursuing a Postgraduate Diploma in Education and Training at the Vocational Training Development Institute (VTDI). My teaching practice has taken me across four host institutions in Jamaica — EXCED, UCC, Rockfort Vocational Training Centre, and VTDI itself — where I have documented over 229 hours of teaching experience.

My professional background is rooted in Information Technology. I hold a Bachelor of Science in Information Technology and am currently pursuing a Master of Science at the University of the West Indies (UWI). This foundation allows me to bring real-world ICT knowledge into my teaching, making lessons relevant, practical, and connected to industry needs.

I am drawn to teaching because I believe that education — particularly in technology — has the power to open doors. I approach every classroom with the same curiosity and preparation I bring to my professional work, and I am committed to growing as an educator throughout this programme and beyond.
